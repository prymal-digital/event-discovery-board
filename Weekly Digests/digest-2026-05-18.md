# Where to be this week — Mon 18–Sun 24 May 2026

Pentecost weekend lands midweek. Belgium gets the festival heat (Extrema Outdoor, Jazz Middelheim, free jazz in Brussels). Amsterdam answers with Das Festival on Saturday. Barcelona stays steady with Nitsa and a Boris Brejcha-headlined Brunch on Sunday. Plan around Friday–Sunday — Mon/Tue/Wed are for AI demos and ecstatic recovery.

## The 5 picks

- **Sat 12:00 — Extrema Outdoor Belgium Day 2** *(Houthalen-Helchteren)* — Score 9.3
  Forest, lake, beach. Adam Beyer, Tale of Us, Amelie Lens, ARTBAT, Marco Carola, Kölsch. The Saturday for which you block this whole long weekend. [extrema.be](https://www.extrema.be/en/)

- **Fri 12:00 — Extrema Outdoor Belgium Day 1** *(Houthalen-Helchteren)* — Score 9.2
  Pentecost-weekend opener of Belgium's most beautiful electronic festival. If you can only do one day, do this or Saturday — not Sunday. [extrema.be](https://www.extrema.be/en/)

- **Sat 12:00 — komm schon Alter "Das Festival"** *(Amsterdam, Tuinen van West)* — Score 9.0
  NTO live, Stephan Bodzin live, Eelke Kleijn, Einmusik, Andhim. Melodic-techno by daylight in the green pockets of West. If Belgium is too far, this is the call. [RA ↗](https://ra.co/events/2311679)

- **Sun 12:00 — Extrema Outdoor Sunday Closer** *(Houthalen-Helchteren)* — Score 9.0
  Long sets, sunset stages, the day to actually stop and listen between dancefloors. Pair with the Pentecost-Monday public holiday. [extrema.be](https://www.extrema.be/en/line-up/sunday/schedule)

- **Sun 16:00 — Brunch Electronik BCN: Boris Brejcha · Joyhauser · Magdalena** *(Barcelona, Poble Espanyol)* — Score 8.8
  Brunch moves to Poble Espanyol this Sunday (not Fòrum). Brejcha's dark high-energy thing in daylight on a Pentecost Sunday — predictable but right. [barcelona.brunchelectronik.com ↗](https://barcelona.brunchelectronik.com/)

## Don't miss if you're in...

**Amsterdam.** Tue: AI Tinkerers May Edition at NIO House (live AI demos, 8.5) and Ecstatic Dance @ Loods12 (8.5). Fri: Shelter w/ Julian Anthony, Retromigration & Soul Mass Transit System (8.0). Sun morning: Ecstatic Dance @ The Other Side (8.5) — the right reset.

**Barcelona.** Fri: Nitsa w/ Erol Alkan (8.5). Sat: Nitsa w/ Efdemin · Konduku (8.5) — hypnotic, dubby, deep-end techno for cerebral Saturdays.

**Antwerp.** Sat–Sun: Jazz Middelheim at Park Den Brandt — Cécile McLorin Salvant, Cymande, Hunee, Marcos Valle, Buena Vista Orchestra (8.0 / 7.8). Belgium's flagship outdoor jazz at its peak.

**Brussels.** Fri–Sun: Lotto Brussels Jazz Weekend — six city squares, brass parade, free (8.0 / 7.8). Easiest culture hit in town all year.
