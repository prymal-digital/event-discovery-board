# Where to be this week — Mon 18–Sun 24 May 2026

Pentecost long weekend is the gravity well. Belgium delivers the heat: Extrema Outdoor's full three-day run, Fuse and C12 holding the Brussels night, Jazz Middelheim in Antwerp, free jazz across Brussels' squares. Amsterdam answers with **Das Festival** Saturday and a fat club Saturday (Thuishaven daytime → Garage Noord / TILLATEC night). Barcelona stays steady: Nitsa Fri+Sat, Brunch Electronik with Boris Brejcha closing Sunday. Monday–Wednesday: AI demos, ecstatic dance recovery, the rest of the week is a festival weekend.

## The 5 picks

- **Sat 12:00 — Extrema Outdoor Belgium Day 2** *(Houthalen-Helchteren)* — Score 9.3
  Adam Beyer, Tale of Us, Amelie Lens, ARTBAT, Marco Carola, Kölsch. Forest + lake + beach. The Saturday for which you block this whole long weekend. [extrema.be ↗](https://www.extrema.be/en/)

- **Fri 12:00 — Extrema Outdoor Belgium Day 1** *(Houthalen-Helchteren)* — Score 9.2
  Pentecost-weekend opener of Belgium's most beautiful electronic festival. If you can only do one day, do Friday or Saturday — not Sunday. [extrema.be ↗](https://www.extrema.be/en/)

- **Sat 12:00 — komm schon Alter "Das Festival"** *(Amsterdam, Tuinen van West)* — Score 9.0
  NTO live, Stephan Bodzin live, Eelke Kleijn, Einmusik, Andhim. Melodic-techno by daylight in the green pockets of West. If Belgium is too far, this is the call. [RA ↗](https://ra.co/events/2311679)

- **Sun 16:00 — Brunch Electronik BCN: Boris Brejcha · Joyhauser · Magdalena** *(Barcelona, Poble Espanyol)* — Score 8.8
  Brunch moves to Poble Espanyol this Sunday (not Fòrum). Brejcha's dark high-energy thing in daylight on a Pentecost Sunday — predictable but right. [brunch electronik ↗](https://barcelona.brunchelectronik.com/)

- **Sat 14:00 — Thuishaven Open-Air: Mahmut Orhan · ARYMÉ · Nils Hoffmann** *(Amsterdam)* — Score 8.5 — SOLD OUT
  Saturday daytime open-air in Thuishaven's post-industrial garden. Sold out — hunt resale. If you snag one, this beats every club. [RA ↗](https://ra.co/events/2294452)
